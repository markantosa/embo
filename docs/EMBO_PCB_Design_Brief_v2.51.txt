======================================================================
1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW
======================================================================
EMBO is a closed-loop embolic agent preparation device. The MCU board integrates three sensor subsystems (UAS, CV camera, StallGuard viscosity), two stepper motor actuators, two mechanical limit switches, and a UI breakout board connected via IDC ribbon cable.

1.1 Why ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8
---------------------------
Module: chip, 8MB flash, 40MHz crystal, PCB antenna — no external MCU support components needed
Built-in USB Serial/JTAG on GPIO19/20 — no bridge IC needed
Bluetooth LE 5.0 on-chip — NimBLE for wireless debug
36 GPIO broken out — 28 used in this design, 8 free. N8 variant: GPIO35–37 free for SPI bus.

1.2 Power rails
---------------

Rail    |  Source           |  Voltage   |  Consumers                                 |  Max current
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12/24V  |  PSU direct       |  12–24V    |  2× TMC2209 module VM                      |  6A peak    
5V      |  LM2596 buck      |  5V ±2%    |  LED panel, TMC2209 module VCC_IO, buzzer  |  2A         
3.3V    |  AMS1117-3.3 LDO  |  3.3V ±1%  |  ESP32, AD9833, IC1/IC2, TFT logic         |  800mA

5. UAS SIGNAL CHAIN
======================================================================
1MHz acoustic chain on 3.3V rail. AD9833 sweepable 0.5–3MHz in firmware.

5.1 AD9833BRMZ — signal generator — CORRECTED in v2.51
------------------------------------------------------
CORRECTION from v2.1–v2.5: three errors removed. (1) RSET pin does not exist on the AD9833 MSOP-10 — it belongs to the AD9834. The 6.8kΩ 1% resistor and 604mVpp RSET-based amplitude calculation are removed from all tables and BOM. (2) The pin previously called "COMP" is correctly named CAP/2.5V (pin 3, per AD9833 Rev G datasheet) — this is the output of an internal 2.5V regulator used by the digital core, and requires a 100nF decoupling cap to DGND, not AGND. (3) The 200Ω external load resistor at VOUT is removed — per Rev G datasheet, an external load resistor is not required because the device has a 200Ω resistor on-board. VOUT connects directly to the AC coupling cap, then to the Tx amp.

Parameter             |  Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IC                    |  AD9833BRMZ (MSOP-10)                                                                                                                                                                                                           
Supply                |  3.3V — both VDD pins (check module pinout for pin numbers)                                                                                                                                                                     
SPI                   |  GPIO38 (FSYNC), GPIO36 (SCLK), GPIO35 (SDATA). Write-only. SPI Mode 2 (CPOL=1, CPHA=0).                                                                                                                                        
CAP/2.5V pin (pin 3)  |  100nF X7R 0402 to DGND — internal 2.5V digital core regulator bypass. Must be connected; do not leave floating. Per Rev G datasheet: DGND, not AGND.                                                                           
VOUT (pin 10)         |  Sine wave output. Amplitude approximately 0.5Vpp at 3.3V supply — fixed internally by the DAC, no external amplitude-setting resistor. Internal 200Ω output impedance — no external load resistor required or should be fitted.
VOUT to Tx amp        |  100nF X7R 0402 AC coupling cap from VOUT directly to IC1 ch-A non-inverting input. No intermediate resistor.                                                                                                                   
Decoupling            |  100nF X7R 0402 per VDD pin + 10µF X5R 0805 bulk                                                                                                                                                                                

The AD9833 output amplitude at 3.3V supply (~0.5Vpp) should be verified on the actual board with an oscilloscope before relying on the UAS attenuation measurement, since the internal amplitude is temperature- and supply-dependent. It is not adjustable in firmware.

5.2 Tx amplifier — IC1 ch-A, G = 4.9
------------------------------------
NOTE: Tx amp gain was originally calculated to prevent clipping based on an incorrect 604mVpp RSET-derived amplitude. With the corrected AD9833 output of approximately 0.5Vpp: G=4.9 → output ≈2.45Vpp. This remains well within the 3.3V supply and the gain resistor values (Rf=390Ω, Rg=100Ω) are unchanged.

Parameter   |  Requirement                                                                      
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IC          |  OPA2354 SOIC-8, ch-A of IC1. 250MHz GBW.                                         
Bias        |  Two 10kΩ (3.3V → non-inv → GND) — 1.65V mid-rail                                 
Input       |  100nF AC coupling from AD9833 VOUT. Input amplitude ≈0.5Vpp.                     
Gain        |  Rf = 390Ω 1%, Rg = 100Ω 1%. G = 4.9. Output ≈ 2.45Vpp. Well within 3.3V supply. ✓
Output      |  47Ω series + 100nF coupling to J1 SMA                                            
Decoupling  |  100nF within 2mm V+ + 10µF bulk

6. MOTOR DRIVERS — 2× BIGTREETECH TMC2209 V1.X PLUG-IN MODULES
======================================================================
U5/U6 are BTT TMC2209 V1.x plug-in modules in 2×8 sockets. Eliminates QFN-28 thermal pad failure mode; field-replaceable.
FUNCTIONAL SENSOR INPUT: StallGuard SG_RESULT feeds the PID mixing-stop algorithm. Depends on sense resistor, coil current, and SpreadCycle mode.

6.1 Module specifications
-------------------------
Fixed onboard sense resistor: 110mΩ 1%, V1.1–V1.3 consistent. Same class as original RS1–RS4.
Current set via onboard trimpot by default — firmware should use UART IHOLD_IRUN with I_scale_analog=0 for repeatability.

-------------------

---------------------------------
VERIFIED: All BTT TMC2209 V1.1/V1.2/V1.3 modules route active UART to 4th pin down (left column), labeled PDN. 5th pin is spare — route only 4th pin, leave 5th NC.

Component  |  Value      |  Connection          
------------------------------------------------
R_UART     |  1kΩ 0402   |  GPIO4 → PDN junction
R_PDN_UP   |  10kΩ 0402  |  PDN junction → 3.3V 


6.4 SpreadCycle — firmware-enforced
-----------------------------------
Module has NO SPREAD pin. SPREAD pull-ups REMOVED from BOM. Firmware must write GCONF.en_spreadcycle=1 at every boot and read back to confirm. Load test on real hardware required before trusting SG_RESULT for PID.

6.5 CLK pin correction
----------------------
CLK must tie to GND for internal oscillator — not float. Two 0Ω resistors (or direct traces) to GND.

6.6 VDD pin
-----------
3.3V assumed safe — not confirmed from module primary source. Verify before first power-on.

10. PCB LAYOUT RULES
======================================================================

10.1 General
------------
2-layer FR4 ENIG. UAS analog top layer only, no vias in UAS paths. Antenna keepout 15mm all layers.

10.2 Power section
------------------
Q1/D7 within 3mm. SS34A within 5mm of SW pin. AMS1117 tab: ≥100mm² copper + ≥4 thermal vias.

10.3 UAS chain
--------------
IC1/IC2: ≥15mm separation. C_adc within 2mm of GPIO1 pad. C_env within 5mm of IC2 ch-B output.
AD9833 VOUT connects via 100nF AC cap directly to IC1 ch-A non-inv input — no intermediate resistor.

10.4 TMC2209 sockets
--------------------
SK1/SK2: hand-solderable 2×8 sockets. VM bulk caps on main board near sockets. CLK GND tie within 5mm of each socket position.

10.5 IDC ribbon and USB-C
-------------------------
J9 at board edge. 100nF at TOUCH_IRQ trace near J9.
D+/D−: A6+B6 single net, A7+B7 single net. CC1/CC2 within 5mm.
