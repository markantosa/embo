Moving average — cheapest to reason about, adds ~window/2 samples of lag. Good default.
EMA (exponential moving average) — one multiply-add per sample, no buffer needed at all — the cheapest option to actually run in the firmware's control loop, and tunable from "barely smoothed" to "heavy" with a single alpha constant.
Median — the only one of the three that rejects sharp single-sample spikes (e.g. a mechanical knock on a load cell, or an occasional bad ADC read) instead of just averaging them in. Costs a small sort every sample, still trivial on an ESP32-S3.
"Noise reduction" below each chart is raw stddev ÷ filtered stddev over the last ~60 samples on screen — a rough, session-local number to help compare parameter choices, not a calibrated metric.
